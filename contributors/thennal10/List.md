# Thennal

Small set of automations I've made. PC automations have their own packs, NPC automations are clubbed together. Generally, I focus on automations for stuff that I've found annoying to do in-game, particularly tracking stuff (e.g the Mimic Gun values) and reactions in general. Neither the PC nor NPC automations are fully comprehensive for their respective mechs. The LA version indicated is the latest tested version, not neccesarily the minimum compatible version.
